# Infographic sources

Every design here comes from the "01-MASSIVE PowerPoint Infographics" pack, purchased with a
licence for this use. The purchase receipt is held with the source files. Each design's
`source` in `manifest.json` names its original deck and slide.

The designs were prepared by `tools/pptx-infographics/build.py` from the picks in
`tools/pptx-infographics/picks-v1.json`:
- the sample title was removed
- sizes were scaled to 13.333 × 7.5 in
- colours that matched the source theme were turned into theme slots
- fonts were set to Calibri, with Nikosh for Bangla
- the text slots were named

Rebuild them with that command; do not edit the files by hand.
