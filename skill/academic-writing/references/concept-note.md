# Concept note (UGC, ministry or funder)

A short pitch for funding: usually **2–3 pages** (about 1,000–1,500 words), formal, third person,
deliver as Word. Use the funder's template or call text when supplied. Amounts, dates and names come
from the user; otherwise leave `[amount]`, `[date]`, `[name]` and list them in the reply.

| Section | What goes in it | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Title page block | Project title, submitting institution, contact person, date, total indicative cost. | — |
| 1. Background and rationale | The problem, its scale (the user's figures or cited sources), the policy link (national policy, UGC strategic plan, SDG) and why this institution. | 250–400 words |
| 2. Objectives | One overall objective; 3–5 specific, measurable objectives. | bullets |
| 3. Project components | 3–5 components (e.g. infrastructure, capacity building, curriculum, research, digital systems), each with its key activities. | 1 short paragraph each |
| 4. Beneficiaries | Direct and indirect, with numbers where known (students, staff, institutions, community). | 80–150 words |
| 5. Implementation period and arrangement | Duration, start, phases or a simple quarter-by-quarter table; implementing unit and steering committee. | table or 100 words |
| 6. Indicative cost by component | Table below; currency stated; total matches the title block. | table |
| 7. Expected outcomes | Outputs and outcomes, each with an indicator and target. | bullets or table |
| 8. Sustainability | Who runs and funds it after the project; maintenance; institutionalisation. | 80–150 words |
| Optional | Risks and mitigation; monitoring and evaluation; alignment with funder priorities. | — |

## Common reviewer complaints

- Background is generic; no evidence of the need at this institution.
- Components are lists of purchases, not linked to objectives.
- Costs not broken down, or total differs between sections.
- Outcomes not measurable; no sustainability plan after funding ends.
