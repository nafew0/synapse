# Formats and their sources

Tags: **[P]** read on a published document of that body. **[C]** common practice; no primary source
was found, so an uploaded example from the user's office beats it. Phrases are short excerpts; open
the source to read the whole document.

## Circular, Sri Lanka, Public Administration [P]
Source: Circular 02/2021(VIII), https://www.ugc.ac.lk/attachments/2453_PA%20Cicular%20-02-2021-VIII-e.pdf

A4, Times New Roman 12. `Public Administration Circular : 02/2021(VIII)` top left. Right block: My
No, ministry, address, date `dd.mm.yyyy`. Addressees one per line, no salutation. Subject bold,
size 14, centred, underlined, no label. First paragraph unnumbered, then `02.` `03.` Signature right:
name, Secretary, Ministry, then telephone, fax and email.

## Circular, UGC Sri Lanka [P]
Sources: Establishments Circular Letter 08/2023; Commission Circular 913.

Centred crest and bold name. Bold number on the left, address on the right of the same line. Date on
the right, `03rd October, 2023`. Addressees left, no salutation. Subject bold capitals underlined,
left. Unnumbered justified paragraphs. Signature left: bold name, post. `Copies:` numbered. `Contd./`
between pages. Last line `File No:`.

## Covering letter, Sri Lankan university [P]
Source: University of Moratuwa, https://www.uom.lk/sites/default/files/inline-files/Coveritng%20letter-%20%20English.pdf

Printed letterhead with My No, Your No and Date. Addressees by post. Bold underlined capital subject.
No salutation, no numbering, no close. Signature left. The office uses printed paper, so leave out
`letterhead` when they do.

## Letter to students or staff, Sri Lankan university [P]
Sources: Eastern University, https://www.fsc.esn.ac.lk/sites/default/files/2022-04/Student%20Enrolment_1.pdf ; PGIM circular letter, https://pgim.cmb.ac.lk/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/Cir-1-1.pdf

My No, `Dear Student,`, capital subject, no close. PGIM uses `To :` and paragraphs numbered from 1.

## Letter, Bangladesh, in English [P]
Source: UGC Bangladesh, 31.07.2024, https://ugc.gov.bd/storage/upload/service-table-data/20260702/20260702_IaeYCdq98NS3KzrsqJai.pdf

Centred `Government of the People's Republic of Bangladesh`, body in bold, address, website, rule.
`Ref. No.` left and `Date:` right on one line. Bold `Subject:` before `Dear Sir;`. First paragraph
unnumbered, then bold `02.`; sub-items `(a)`. `Enclosure:` left, with `With the approval of the
competent authority,` on the right. Signature right. `Distribution:` list. Copy heading as in the
house. `Page - 01 of 02` footer.

## Office order, Bangladesh [P]
Sources: Bangladesh Open University, https://bou.ac.bd/upload/notice/officeorder/1880353123996109.pdf ; Jagannath University, https://jnu.ac.bd/assets/notice/c5f8605e738ea15d709c64cb3ba48f7b.pdf ; Dhaka University notice, https://cmc.du.ac.bd/notices/620/notice_1653288558.pdf

Bilingual letterhead, memo number left, date right, centred `Office Order`. `Copy forwarded for
information & necessary action:` numbered, ending `Personal file.` Jagannath uses `Distributed to:`.
