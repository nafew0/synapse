You use Synapse to think, write and work, so we want you to know exactly what happens to what you put into it. This page says it in plain language.

1. What we collect

What you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, username, profile picture and institution. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Your content: the messages you write, the files and images you attach, and what Synapse creates for you.
  • Voice: a recording from the microphone button is sent to a speech-to-text service to become text. We do not keep the recording. Audio files you attach yourself are treated like any other file.
  • Feedback: ratings you give a reply, and what you send with “Report an issue”.

What is recorded as you use it

  • Usage records: which model you used, token counts and quota use. They do not contain your content.
  • Technical logs: time, request path, errors and IP address, used to run and secure the service.
  • Quality traces: a record of each AI run (your prompt, the reply and the tools used) linked to your user and institution, used to find and fix problems. They are kept for a short time only (see How long we keep data).
  • Sign-in data: session and refresh tokens, and your last sign-in time.

From your institution

Your institution administrator may invite you or create your account, giving us your name, email address and role.

2. How we use your data

  • To answer you, run the tools you ask for (search the web, edit a document, run code) and keep your history.
  • To sign you in, protect accounts and prevent abuse.
  • To apply usage limits and show your institution usage figures.
  • To find and fix errors, using traces and the reports you send.
  • To email you about your account, such as invitations and password resets.

Sending a message, attaching a file or pressing the microphone is your consent to process that content to answer you. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting the content, deleting your account, or writing to us. We do not use your data for profiling, behavioural tracking or advertising.

3. Who else handles your data

Synapse works with a few services to do what you ask. We send each one only what it needs.

  • AI model providers, through OpenRouter. Your prompts and attached files go to the model you pick. We choose settings that stop providers from training on prompts and, where possible, from keeping them. [Ops: confirm once the OpenRouter no-training and zero-retention settings are on.]
  • Speech-to-text (Grok, through OpenRouter). Your recording, only when you use the microphone.
  • Web search (Firecrawl). Search queries and page addresses, only when the Office Assistant looks something up for you.
  • Code and file tools. The files you work on run in a BdREN-run sandbox and are kept there for a short time.
  • Quality traces (Langfuse), run by BdREN. Prompts, replies and your user and institution identifiers.
  • Email. Our mail server sends invitations, password resets and receives issue reports.

Some AI providers are outside Bangladesh, so the part of a request they handle leaves the country. Please do not enter sensitive personal data you are not allowed to share with a third party, such as health records, biometric data, financial account details, national ID numbers, or personal data about children.

We also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety and security of users and the service.

5. Your rights and controls

  • Delete a conversation from the sidebar. Its files and document text go with it.
  • Delete your account from Settings. This removes your conversations, files and profile.
  • Use a temporary chat when you do not want a conversation kept.
  • Access and correct: ask what we hold about you and have mistakes fixed.
  • Object or withdraw consent: tell us to stop using your data for a purpose, and we will, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Erasure and export: ask us to delete your data or give you a copy.
  • Human review: ask for a person to look at any decision about you that relied on Synapse output.

Write to info@bdren.ai from the email address on your account. We aim to reply within 30 days, at no charge. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the relevant data protection authority, or use the government's Grievance Redressal System under the Cabinet Division or the national helpline. 6. AI and automated decisions

  • Synapse gives suggestions and drafts. It does not make decisions about you with legal or similar significant effect, and BdREN does not use it to grade, rank, score or profile you.
  • Replies can be wrong, so check them before relying on them.
  • We do not train models on your data, so there is nothing of yours to be unlearned. If you delete your data, it is deleted from Synapse as described above.
  • If your institution uses Synapse output in a decision about you, you may ask the institution to explain the main factors behind the outcome and what would have had to be different, and to have a person review it.

7. Your institution

Each institution is kept separate: members of one institution cannot see another institution's data. An institution administrator can add and remove members and see usage figures for the institution. [Legal/ops: state exactly what administrators can see, in particular whether they can read conversation content.] Your institution may also have its own rules for using Synapse.

8. Security

  • Traffic between your browser and Synapse is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and sessions expire automatically.
  • Each institution's data is isolated from the others, and uploaded images require you to be signed in.
  • Access to servers and logs is limited to the BdREN team that runs the service.

No online service is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and your institution as the law requires.

9. Age

Synapse is for members of BdREN institutions. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16 without the consent of a parent or legal guardian. We never use children's data to train models or for commercial purposes, and we do not profile or track children. If you think a child's data was entered without consent, write to us and we will delete it. [Legal: confirm who obtains guardian consent, the institution or BdREN.]

10. Cookies and local storage

Synapse sets only the cookies it needs to keep you signed in: a refresh token valid for up to 7 days, and a session cookie. Your browser also stores settings such as theme and language, and unsent message drafts. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
