Privacy Policy

Operated by BdREN · Version 0.1 (draft) · Effective: [date of approval] · Last updated: 12 October 2026

Synapse is an AI assistant for students, researchers and staff of BdREN member institutions. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it, who else handles it, and how to get it deleted.

DRAFT for BdREN legal review. Every retention period below is the target policy in the data retention plan. Publish only after each one is enforced in production.

1. Our commitments

  • No model training. We do not use your conversations, files or voice recordings to train or fine-tune AI models. We also configure our model providers not to train on your prompts.
  • We do not go through your private data. BdREN staff do not browse or read your conversations or files. Access happens only when needed to fix a fault you report, to keep the service secure, or when the law requires it, and is limited to the people who run the service.
  • Stored on BdREN servers. Your account, conversations and files are stored and processed on servers operated by BdREN. The only data that leaves them is what a model, search or speech tool needs to answer your request, and it is sent to the provider you chose to use for that request.
  • Deleted periodically. Data is removed automatically on a schedule (see How long we keep data), and you can delete it yourself at any time.
  • No sale, no ads. We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your name, email address, username, password (stored only as a hash), profile picture, and your institution.
  • Content: the messages you write, the files and images you attach, and anything you ask Synapse to create.
  • Voice: audio you record with the microphone button, used only to turn your speech into text.
  • Feedback and reports: ratings you give a reply, and the description and technical details you send with “Report an issue”.

Information created as you use Synapse

  • Usage records: which model you used, token counts and quota use. These do not contain your content.
  • Technical logs: time, request path, error messages, and IP address, used to keep the service running and secure.
  • Quality traces: a record of each AI run (your prompt, the model's reply and the tools used), linked to your user and institution identifiers, used to find and fix problems.
  • Sign-in data: session and refresh tokens, and the time of your last sign-in.

Information from your institution

Your institution administrator may invite you or create your account, giving us your name, email address and role.

3. How we use it

  • To answer you, run tools you ask for (search the web, edit a document, run code) and keep your conversation history.
  • To sign you in, protect accounts and prevent abuse.
  • To apply usage limits and show your institution how Synapse is used.
  • To find and fix errors and improve reliability, using traces and the reports you send.
  • To contact you about your account, for example invitations and password resets.

4. Your institution

Each institution is kept separate: members of one institution cannot see another institution's data. An institution administrator can add and remove members and see usage figures for the institution. 6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete a conversation from the sidebar. Its files and document text go with it.
  • Delete your account from Settings. This removes your conversations, files and profile.
  • Use a temporary chat when you do not want a conversation kept.
  • Access, correct or export your data, or object to how we use it: write to info@bdren.ai from the email address on your account. We aim to reply within 30 days.
  • Complain: if you are not satisfied with our reply, you may raise it with the relevant data protection authority. [Legal: confirm the authority and applicable law.]

7. Security

  • Traffic between your browser and Synapse is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Sessions expire automatically.
  • Each institution's data is isolated from the others, and uploaded images require you to be signed in.
  • Access to servers and logs is limited to the BdREN team that runs the service.

No online service is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and your institution as the law requires.

8. Cookies and local storage

Synapse sets only the cookies it needs to keep you signed in (a refresh token valid for up to 7 days, and a session cookie). Your browser also stores settings such as theme and language, and unsent message drafts. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

9. Changes and contact

When we change this policy we update the version and date at the top, and for material changes we will tell you in the app. Questions, requests or complaints: info@bdren.ai.
