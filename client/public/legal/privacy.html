Privacy Policy

Operated by BdREN · Version 0.1 (draft) · Effective date: [to be set on approval]

This policy explains what data Synapse handles, why, and for how long. Draft prepared by engineering from the live configuration; pending BdREN legal review.

1. Who we are

Synapse is operated by the Bangladesh Research and Education Network (BdREN). Contact: info@bdren.ai.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, and the institution you belong to.
  • Conversations, including the files you attach and the files the assistant creates.
  • Voice input, when you use the microphone.
  • Usage records such as token counts and quotas.
  • Technical logs needed to run and secure the service.

6. Your rights

You can delete your conversations and your account from within Synapse. For other requests, including export, write to info@bdren.ai.

7. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit, access is controlled by sign-in, and each institution's data is isolated from the others.

8. Cookies

Synapse uses only the cookies needed to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Changes

We will update the version and effective date above whenever this policy changes.
